How to play

This is Arrow Trails, wrapped around a blueberry. Tap an arrow whose path off the berry is clear. The whole line slides along itself and out in the direction its head points, even if it bends.

An arrow can leave only if the corridor in front of its head is clear — all the way off the berry, including arrows on the far side. The berry itself does not block, only other arrows.

A blocked tap shakes the arrow, highlights what is in the way, and costs 1 heart (4 max). Use a hint if you get stuck. Drag to turn the berry, pinch or use + − to zoom, and ⤢ to fit.
