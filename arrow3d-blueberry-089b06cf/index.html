How to play

This blueberry is a 3D Arrow Trails puzzle. Drag to spin it all the way around, then tap a glowing arrow to slide it off.

An arrow can leave only if the path in the direction of its head is clear — all the way off the berry. Other arrows can block it, even ones on the far side or on a sticking-out step. The berry itself does not block, only arrows.

A blocked tap flashes red and costs a heart. You have three. Use a hint if you get stuck.
