How to play

This blueberry is a dense 3D Arrow Trails puzzle. Drag to spin it, pinch to zoom, then tap a glowing line to slide it off.

An arrow can leave only if the corridor in front of its head is clear — all the way off the berry, including arrows on the far side. The berry itself does not block, only other arrows.

A blocked tap flashes red and costs a heart. You have three. Use a hint if you get stuck.
